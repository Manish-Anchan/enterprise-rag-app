# NovaTech Solutions — Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Last Updated:** February 15, 2025
**Document Owner:** Finance Operations

---

## 1. Overview

This policy governs the reimbursement of business-related expenses incurred by NovaTech Solutions employees. Our goal is to ensure employees are fairly reimbursed for legitimate business expenses while maintaining fiscal responsibility. All employees are expected to exercise good judgment and spend company funds as if they were their own.

## 2. What Is Covered

The following categories of expenses are eligible for reimbursement:

- **Business Travel:** Flights, hotels, ground transportation, and meals incurred during approved business travel (see the Travel Policy for detailed guidelines).
- **Client Meals and Entertainment:** Meals with clients, prospects, or partners for legitimate business purposes.
- **Team Events:** Team dinners, off-sites, social events, and celebrations approved by a manager.
- **Home Office:** Equipment and supplies for your home workspace (beyond the initial $1,500 stipend). Examples include replacement monitors, ergonomic accessories, desk lamps, or whiteboards.
- **Professional Development:** Expenses covered under the $2,000 annual learning budget (courses, conferences, books, certifications).
- **Software and Tools:** Software subscriptions or tools needed for your role that are not part of the standard IT provisioning. Must be pre-approved by your manager and IT.

## 3. Spending Limits

| Category | Limit | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Individual meal (business travel) | $50 per meal | Includes tax and tip |
| Team meal/event | $100 per person | Manager-approved |
| Client entertainment | $150 per person | VP pre-approval for >$500 total |
| Domestic hotel | $250 per night | Excluding taxes and fees |
| International hotel | $350 per night | Excluding taxes and fees |
| Home office supplies | $200 per item | One-off purchases; larger items need manager approval |
| Software subscription | $50 per month | Pre-approval required from IT |

## 4. Approval Chain

Expense approvals follow this hierarchy based on total expense amount:

| Amount | Approver |
|--------|----------|
| Under $500 | Direct Manager |
| $500 — $4,999 | Director |
| $5,000 and above | VP |

For recurring expenses (e.g., monthly software subscriptions), the total annual cost determines the approval level. A $100/month subscription ($1,200/year) requires Director approval.

## 5. Submission Process

1. **Collect Receipts:** Photograph or save digital receipts for all expenses. Itemized receipts are required for any expense over $25. Credit card statements alone are not sufficient.
2. **Submit in Expensify:** Log into Expensify (expensify.novatech.io), create a new expense report, attach receipts, categorize each expense, and add a brief business justification.
3. **Submission Deadline:** Expense reports must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the expense being incurred. Reports submitted after 30 days may be denied.
4. **Manager Review:** Your manager will review and approve the expense report. They may request additional documentation or clarification.

## 6. Reimbursement Timeline

- Approved expense reports are processed on a **bi-weekly** cycle (every other Friday).
- Reimbursements are paid via **direct deposit** to the same account as your payroll.
- Typical turnaround: 5-10 business days after approval.
- International employees: Reimbursements are made in local currency at the exchange rate on the date of approval.

## 7. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses will **not** be reimbursed:

- **Alcohol** — except during client dinners with VP pre-approval, or company-sponsored events with a pre-approved budget.
- **First class or business class flights** — under any circumstances (see Travel Policy).
- **Personal expenses** — clothing, personal grooming, personal travel extensions, or entertainment not related to business.
- **Traffic violations and parking tickets** — incurred during business travel or commuting.
- **Airline upgrades** — unless medically required with documentation.
- **Spouse or partner travel** — travel expenses for non-employees are not reimbursable.
- **Gift cards** — cannot be purchased with company funds without VP and Finance approval.
- **Political donations** — NovaTech does not reimburse political contributions of any kind.

## 8. Corporate Credit Card

Corporate credit cards (Brex) are available for employees at **L6 and above** or for employees who travel frequently (4 or more trips per quarter).

### Corporate Card Guidelines
- The corporate card should be used **only** for business expenses.
- All corporate card transactions must still be submitted through Expensify with receipts.
- Personal charges on the corporate card must be repaid within 10 business days.
- Lost or stolen cards must be reported immediately to Finance Operations at finance@novatech.io.
- Card limits are set based on role and typical spending patterns. Contact Finance to request a limit adjustment.

## 9. Mileage Reimbursement

If you use your personal vehicle for business travel (not regular commuting), you may claim mileage reimbursement at the current IRS standard rate ($0.67 per mile for 2025). Log the date, starting location, destination, business purpose, and total miles in Expensify.

## 10. Policy Violations

Submitting fraudulent, inflated, or personal expenses for reimbursement is a serious violation and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. If you are unsure whether an expense qualifies, ask your manager or Finance Operations before incurring it.

For questions about expense reimbursement, contact Finance Operations at finance@novatech.io or post in the #finance-questions Slack channel.

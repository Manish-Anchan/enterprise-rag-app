# NovaTech Solutions: Acceptable Use Policy

**Last Updated:** February 20, 2025
**Document Owner:** IT Operations

## 1. Introduction
The Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) establishes the rules and expectations for the appropriate use of NovaTech Solutions' computing resources, networks, and communication systems. This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, and temporary staff utilizing corporate assets. As a mid-sized organization of 500 employees, ensuring the secure and responsible use of our technology infrastructure is critical to maintaining operational efficiency and safeguarding company data.

## 2. Company Devices
NovaTech provides employees with computing devices (laptops, phones) necessary to perform their job functions. 
- **Primary Use:** Company devices are intended strictly for work-related activities.
- **Software Installation:** Employees are prohibited from installing personal, unapproved, or unlicensed software on company devices. All software requests must go through IT Operations for review and approval.
- **Device Security:** Devices must remain secured. Employees must not disable installed security software (e.g., antivirus, endpoint detection) or alter device configurations (e.g., jailbreaking, rooting).

## 3. Internet Usage
The company network provides access to the internet to facilitate business operations.
- **Reasonable Personal Use:** NovaTech permits reasonable, limited personal use of the internet during designated break times, provided it does not interfere with productivity, consume excessive bandwidth, or violate other company policies.
- **Content Filtering:** IT Operations employs content filtering solutions to block access to malicious websites, illegal content, adult material, and bandwidth-intensive streaming services not related to business functions. Attempting to bypass these filters is a direct violation of this policy.

## 4. Email and Communications
Corporate email and messaging platforms (e.g., Slack) are official business communication channels.
- **Professionalism:** All communications must maintain a professional tone and comply with the company's anti-harassment and code of conduct policies.
- **Prohibited Forwarding:** Employees are strictly prohibited from auto-forwarding corporate emails to personal email accounts (e.g., Gmail, Yahoo).
- **Data Loss Prevention (DLP):** All outbound email is subject to automated DLP scanning to detect and prevent the unauthorized transmission of sensitive, Confidential, or Restricted data.

## 5. Cloud Storage and Collaboration
Secure collaboration is essential for our modern workforce.
- **Approved Platforms:** Employees must use approved corporate cloud storage solutions (OneDrive, Google Drive for Business) for storing and sharing company data.
- **Prohibited Services:** The use of personal cloud storage accounts (e.g., personal Dropbox, iCloud) for storing, processing, or transmitting NovaTech data is strictly forbidden.

## 6. Social Media Usage
Employees must exercise judgment and responsibility when engaging on social media platforms.
- **Company Representation:** Employees may not speak on behalf of NovaTech Solutions, post official company announcements, or engage in PR activities on social media without explicit, prior written approval from the Marketing Department.
- **Confidentiality:** Discussing unreleased products, financial data, internal operational details, or client information on social media is prohibited.

## 7. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)
NovaTech recognizes that some employees prefer to use personal mobile devices for work-related tasks, such as checking email or Slack.
- **MDM Enrollment:** Personal devices used for business purposes must be enrolled in the company's Mobile Device Management (MDM) solution.
- **Corporate Control:** By enrolling a personal device, the employee grants NovaTech IT the authority to enforce security policies (e.g., device PIN requirements) and the right to remotely wipe corporate data from the device in the event it is lost, stolen, or upon termination of employment.

## 8. Monitoring and Privacy
NovaTech Solutions respects employee privacy but must balance this with the need to protect corporate assets.
- **Right to Monitor:** The company reserves the right to monitor, log, and audit all usage of corporate networks, devices, internet access, and communication systems. This includes analyzing traffic patterns, inspecting device contents, and reviewing communication logs.
- **No Expectation of Privacy:** Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company-owned equipment or networks, even for permitted personal use.

## 9. Policy Violations and Disciplinary Action
Compliance with this Acceptable Use Policy is a condition of employment at NovaTech Solutions. Violations are taken seriously and will be handled according to the following progressive disciplinary process:
1. **Counseling/Verbal Warning:** For minor, unintentional infractions, the employee will receive coaching from their manager and IT.
2. **Written Warning:** Repeated or more significant violations will result in a formal written warning placed in the employee's HR file.
3. **Termination:** Severe violations (e.g., intentional data exfiltration, viewing illegal content, repeated insubordination regarding IT policies) may result in immediate suspension, termination of employment, and potential legal action.

## 10. Conclusion and Employee Acknowledgment
All new hires are required to sign an acknowledgment of this policy during onboarding. Periodic re-acknowledgments may be requested when major updates are published. The IT Operations team, in partnership with People Operations, oversees the administration and enforcement of these terms. Any uncertainties about the acceptability of a specific practice or software tool should be brought to the immediate attention of the IT Helpdesk before action is taken.

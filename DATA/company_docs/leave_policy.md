# NovaTech Solutions — Leave & Time-Off Policy

**Last Updated:** February 1, 2025
**Document Owner:** People Operations

---

## 1. Overview

NovaTech Solutions believes that rest and time away from work are essential for sustained high performance and employee well-being. This policy outlines the leave benefits available to all eligible employees. All full-time employees (FTEs) working 30 or more hours per week are eligible for the benefits described in this document. Part-time employees and contractors should refer to their individual agreements for leave provisions.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO)

### Allocation
All full-time employees receive **20 days (160 hours) of PTO per calendar year**, regardless of level or tenure. There is no distinction between vacation days and personal days — PTO can be used for any purpose.

### Accrual
PTO accrues at a rate of **1.67 days (13.33 hours) per month**. New employees begin accruing PTO from their first day of employment. PTO is available for use as it accrues; there is no waiting period.

### Rollover
Employees may roll over a maximum of **5 unused PTO days (40 hours)** into the next calendar year. Any PTO balance exceeding 5 days as of December 31 will be forfeited. We strongly encourage employees to use their full PTO allocation each year. Managers should proactively remind their team members to take time off.

### PTO Upon Termination
Unused, accrued PTO will be paid out upon voluntary or involuntary termination of employment, in accordance with applicable state law.

## 3. Sick Leave

All full-time employees receive **10 sick days (80 hours) per calendar year**. Sick leave is for use when an employee is ill, injured, or needs to attend medical appointments. It may also be used to care for an ill immediate family member (spouse, child, parent).

### Documentation
- For absences of **fewer than 3 consecutive days**, no doctor's note or medical documentation is required. Simply notify your manager via Slack or email before your scheduled start time.
- For absences of **3 or more consecutive days**, a doctor's note confirming the need for leave may be requested by People Operations.

### Accrual and Rollover
Sick leave does not accrue; the full 10-day allocation is available on January 1 of each year (or on the hire date for new employees, prorated). Unused sick leave does **not** roll over and is **not** paid out upon termination.

## 4. Parental Leave

NovaTech Solutions provides generous parental leave to support employees welcoming a new child through birth, adoption, or foster care placement.

- **Primary Caregiver:** 16 weeks of fully paid leave. The primary caregiver is defined as the parent who will be the primary provider of care for the child during the leave period.
- **Secondary Caregiver:** 8 weeks of fully paid leave.
- **Eligibility:** Employees must have been employed for at least 6 months at the time the leave begins.
- **Flexibility:** Parental leave may be taken consecutively or split into two blocks within the first 12 months following the child's birth or placement. Arrangements must be coordinated with the employee's manager and People Operations.
- **Return to Work:** Employees returning from parental leave will return to the same position or an equivalent role. A gradual ramp-back schedule (e.g., reduced hours for the first 2 weeks) is available upon request.

## 5. Bereavement Leave

NovaTech Solutions provides paid bereavement leave to employees who experience the loss of a loved one.

- **Immediate Family:** 5 days of paid bereavement leave. Immediate family is defined as spouse or domestic partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, or grandchild.
- **Extended Family:** 3 days of paid bereavement leave. Extended family includes aunts, uncles, cousins, in-laws, and close family friends.
- Additional time off may be taken using PTO or unpaid leave, with manager approval.

## 7. Jury Duty and Voting

- **Jury Duty:** Employees summoned for jury duty will receive full pay for up to 10 business days. Provide a copy of your jury summons to People Operations. If jury duty extends beyond 10 days, additional time will be unpaid but your job is protected.
- **Voting:** Employees may take up to 2 hours of paid time off to vote in federal, state, or local elections if polling hours do not allow sufficient time outside working hours.

## 8. Requesting Time Off

### Process
1. Submit your time-off request through **Workday** (workday.novatech.io) at least **2 weeks in advance** for requests of 3 or more consecutive days. For shorter requests, 48 hours notice is appreciated.
2. Your manager will review and approve or discuss the request within **48 hours**.
3. Once approved, the time off will automatically appear on your team's shared calendar.

### Guidelines
- **No blackout periods:** NovaTech does not enforce company-wide blackout dates. However, we ask that employees coordinate with their teams to ensure adequate coverage, especially during critical project milestones or release cycles.
- **Courtesy notice:** For PTO requests exceeding 3 consecutive days, we ask for at least **2 weeks advance notice** as a courtesy to your team.
- **Peak periods:** During quarter-end or major releases, managers may ask employees to coordinate timing to ensure minimum team coverage. This is handled on a case-by-case basis.

## 9. Unpaid Leave

In exceptional circumstances, employees may request unpaid leave of absence for up to 30 days. Requests must be submitted to People Operations and approved by both the employee's manager and VP. Benefits continue during approved unpaid leave of up to 30 days.

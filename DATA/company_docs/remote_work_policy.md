# NovaTech Solutions — Remote Work & WFH Policy

**Last Updated:** April 1, 2025
**Document Owner:** People Operations

---

## 1. Overview

NovaTech Solutions embraces a hybrid work model that balances the benefits of in-person collaboration with the flexibility of remote work. We believe that great work can happen anywhere, and we trust our employees to manage their time and deliver results. This policy outlines the guidelines and expectations for all employees regarding remote and hybrid work arrangements.

## 2. Hybrid Work Schedule

All full-time employees follow a hybrid schedule of **3 days in-office and 2 days remote per week**.

- **Mandatory In-Office Days:** Tuesday and Thursday are designated as mandatory in-office days for all hybrid employees. These days are specifically chosen to facilitate team collaboration, cross-functional meetings, sprint planning sessions, and in-person social interaction.
- **Flexible In-Office Day:** The third in-office day is determined by each team's preference and should be consistent week-to-week. Most teams choose Monday or Wednesday. Coordinate with your manager and team to establish your third day.
- **Remote Days:** The remaining 2 days are work-from-home days. You may choose which days work best for you, subject to team coordination.

## 3. Fully Remote Eligibility

Employees who meet the following criteria may apply for fully remote status:

- **Tenure:** Must have completed at least **6 months** of employment at NovaTech Solutions.
- **Performance:** Must have received a "Meets Expectations" or higher rating on their most recent performance review.
- **Approval:** Requires sign-off from both the direct manager and the relevant VP.
- **Quarterly Visits:** Fully remote employees are expected to travel to their assigned office at least **once per quarter** for team events, planning sessions, and in-person collaboration. Travel costs are covered by the company.

To apply for fully remote status, submit a request through Workday and discuss it with your manager.

## 4. Home Office Equipment

NovaTech Solutions supports employees in creating a productive and ergonomic home workspace.

### One-Time Home Office Stipend
All employees receive a **one-time $1,500 stipend** upon starting employment (or upon this policy taking effect for existing employees) to purchase home office equipment. Recommended purchases include an ergonomic desk, an adjustable chair, a monitor, keyboard, mouse, lighting, or a webcam. Receipts must be submitted through Expensify within 60 days. Equipment purchased with this stipend becomes the property of the employee.

### Monthly Internet Reimbursement
All employees receive a **$50 per month internet reimbursement** to offset the cost of home internet service. This is automatically added to your monthly paycheck and does not require receipt submission.

### Company Equipment
Your company-issued laptop (MacBook Pro 14" M3 Pro or equivalent) and any peripherals provided by NovaTech remain company property and must be returned upon termination.

## 5. Core Hours and Availability

Regardless of work location, all employees are expected to be available during **core hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their local timezone**. Core hours ensure sufficient overlap for meetings, synchronous collaboration, and real-time communication.

### Communication Expectations
- **Slack:** Messages should receive a response within **2 hours** during working hours. Set your Slack status to reflect your availability (e.g., "In Focus Mode — back at 2 PM").
- **Calendar:** Keep your Google Calendar up to date. Block time for focused work so colleagues can see your availability. Accept or decline meeting invitations within 24 hours.
- **Video Calls:** Camera on is encouraged but not mandatory for team meetings. For 1:1s with your manager, camera on is expected.
- **Urgent Matters:** For truly urgent matters during core hours, phone calls are preferred over Slack messages. Keep your phone number updated in Workday.

### Flexibility Outside Core Hours
NovaTech measures performance by **output, not hours logged**. You are free to structure your day around your peak productivity hours. If you prefer to start early at 7 AM and wrap up at 3 PM, or start at 11 AM and work until 7 PM, that is acceptable — as long as you are available during core hours and meeting your deliverables.

## 6. Workspace Requirements

When working remotely, employees must ensure:

- **Reliable Internet:** A stable internet connection suitable for video conferencing. Minimum recommended: 25 Mbps download, 10 Mbps upload.
- **Quiet Environment:** A workspace suitable for video calls and focused work. Background noise during meetings should be minimized.
- **Security:** Company devices must be used on secure (password-protected) Wi-Fi networks. Public Wi-Fi (coffee shops, airports) should only be used with VPN active. Screen lock must be enabled when stepping away.

## 7. International Remote Work

Employees may work remotely from **international locations for a maximum of 4 weeks (20 business days) per calendar year**.

### Requirements
- Submit a request through Workday at least **3 weeks in advance**.
- Obtain approval from your manager AND the Legal team. Legal review is required because working from another country may have tax, employment law, and data residency implications.
- You must remain available during your team's core hours (10 AM - 4 PM in your home timezone). If the time zone difference makes this impractical, discuss adjusted hours with your manager before travel.
- Ensure compliance with data handling requirements — some customer data may not be accessed from certain jurisdictions.

### Restrictions
- International remote work from countries under U.S. sanctions is not permitted.
- Stays exceeding 4 weeks require VP and Legal approval and may trigger tax withholding obligations.

## 8. Manager Responsibilities

Managers play a critical role in making hybrid work successful:

- Ensure team meetings are held on in-office days (Tuesday/Thursday) to maximize face time.
- Use asynchronous communication (Confluence docs, Loom videos, Slack threads) for updates that don't require real-time discussion.
- Check in with remote team members regularly to prevent isolation.
- Evaluate performance based on outcomes and deliverables, not presence or hours.
- Approve or discuss remote work arrangement requests within 5 business days.

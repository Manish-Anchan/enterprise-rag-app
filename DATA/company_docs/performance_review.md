# NovaTech Solutions — Performance Review Process

**Last Updated:** January 5, 2025
**Document Owner:** People Operations

---

## 1. Philosophy

At NovaTech Solutions, our performance review process is designed to foster continuous growth, recognize outstanding contributions, and align individual goals with company objectives. Under the guidance of VP of People, David Kim, we believe that regular, constructive feedback is the cornerstone of a high-performing organization. Performance reviews should never be a surprise — they are a structured summary of ongoing conversations between managers and their reports.

## 2. Review Cycle

NovaTech operates on a **semi-annual** formal performance review cycle:

| Cycle | Period Covered | Review Month | Compensation Impact |
|-------|---------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **H1 Review** | January 1 — June 30 | July | Equity refresh grants |
| **H2 Review** | July 1 — December 31 | January | Merit increases, promotions, bonuses |

### Mid-Cycle Check-Ins
Formal reviews should not contain surprises. Managers and employees are required to hold documented **mid-cycle check-ins** in April (for H1) and October (for H2). These check-ins cover progress on goals, roadblocks, development areas, and preliminary feedback.

## 3. Review Process

### Step 1: Self-Assessment (Due 2 weeks before review)
Every employee completes a self-assessment covering:
- Key accomplishments and impact during the review period
- Progress against OKRs and goals set in the previous cycle
- Challenges overcome and lessons learned
- Areas for growth and development goals for the next period
- Any feedback for your manager or team

Self-assessments are submitted through Workday and are visible to your manager and HR Business Partner.

### Step 2: 360-Degree Peer Feedback
- Each employee selects **3 to 5 peers** to provide feedback. Managers approve the peer list to ensure balanced representation (cross-functional collaborators, direct reports if applicable, project partners).
- Peer feedback is **anonymous** — the manager receives aggregated feedback and synthesizes key themes for the review conversation.
- Feedback prompts include: strengths, areas for improvement, collaboration effectiveness, and specific examples of impact.

### Step 3: Manager Evaluation
Managers evaluate each direct report based on:
- Self-assessment and stated accomplishments
- 360-degree peer feedback themes
- Manager's own observations and notes from 1:1s
- Alignment with team and company OKRs
- Demonstration of NovaTech's core values (Transparency, Ownership, Continuous Learning, Customer Obsession)

### Step 4: Performance Rating
Managers assign one of four performance ratings:

| Rating | Description | Typical Distribution |
|--------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Exceeds Expectations** | Consistently performs significantly above level expectations. Delivers exceptional, outsized impact. | ~15% |
| **Meets Expectations** | Reliably meets all expectations for the role and level. Solid, dependable contributor. | ~65% |
| **Developing** | New to the role or actively developing required skills. Making progress but not yet fully meeting level expectations. | ~15% |
| **Below Expectations** | Performance consistently falls short of requirements. Immediate improvement needed. | ~5% |

"Developing" is not inherently negative — it is appropriate for recent hires, newly promoted employees, or those transitioning to significantly new responsibilities.

### Step 5: Calibration Sessions
To ensure fairness and consistency, all ratings undergo calibration:
- Managers meet with peer managers and People Operations in calibration sessions.
- They discuss proposed ratings and rationale to eliminate bias.
- Calibration ensures that "Exceeds Expectations" and other ratings are applied uniformly across teams.
- Calibration may result in rating adjustments based on relative impact and contribution.

### Step 6: Review Conversation
Managers deliver the review in a dedicated 1:1 meeting (minimum 45 minutes). The conversation covers the final rating and rationale, key strengths and impact highlights, development areas with specific examples, goals and expectations for the next period, and career growth aspirations. Both manager and employee sign off on the review in Workday.

## 4. Promotion Criteria

Promotions are earned through demonstrated sustained performance at the next level, not tenure.

### General Criteria (All Levels)
- Must have received "Meets Expectations" or higher for at least **2 consecutive review cycles**.
- Must consistently demonstrate behaviors, scope, and impact commensurate with the next level.
- Manager submits a promotion case with specific evidence of next-level performance.

### Engineering-Specific Criteria by Level

| Promotion | Key Criteria |
|-----------|-------------|
| L3 → L4 | Independently completes features. Writes clean, tested code. Participates in code reviews. |
| L4 → L5 | Owns significant features end-to-end. Mentors L3s. Contributes to design docs. Influences team technical direction. |
| L5 → L6 | Drives technical strategy for a product area. Leads cross-team projects. Recognized as a domain expert. |
| L6 → L7 | Sets technical direction across multiple teams. Influences engineering-wide standards. Executive-level communication. |
| L7 → L8 | Industry-recognized expertise. Shapes company-wide technical strategy. External thought leadership. |

Promotion decisions are finalized by Department VPs and People Operations during the H2 review cycle.

## 5. Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)

If an employee receives a "Below Expectations" rating, or if performance drops significantly between cycles, they may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan.

- **Duration:** Standard PIP lasts **60 days**.
- **Structure:** The PIP document outlines specific, measurable goals that must be achieved. Goals are SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound).
- **Check-Ins:** **Weekly** documented check-ins between the manager and employee are mandatory during the PIP.
- **Support:** The employee receives additional support including more frequent 1:1s, access to mentoring, and prioritized learning resources.
- **Outcome:** If the employee successfully meets PIP objectives, they graduate and return to the standard review cycle. If they do not meet objectives, it may result in role change or termination.

## 6. Compensation Adjustments

### Merit Increases
- Annual merit increases are determined during the **H2 (January)** review cycle.
- Merit increases typically range from **3% to 8%** based on performance rating and position within salary band.
- "Exceeds Expectations" ratings correlate with the upper end of the merit range.
- Employees at or above the top of their salary band may receive a smaller merit increase but are prioritized for promotion.

### Equity Refresh Grants
- High performers ("Exceeds Expectations") are eligible for equity refresh grants.
- Refresh grants are awarded during the February compensation cycle.
- Refresh grants vest over 4 years with no additional cliff.

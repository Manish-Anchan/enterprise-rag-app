# NovaTech Solutions — Company Overview & Culture

**Last Updated:** March 15, 2025
**Document Owner:** People Operations

---

## 1. Mission Statement

NovaTech Solutions exists to empower engineering teams worldwide with cloud infrastructure tools that are powerful yet intuitive. We believe that great developer tools should remove friction, not add it — enabling teams to ship faster, scale confidently, and focus on building products their customers love.

## 2. Vision

To become the most trusted enterprise cloud infrastructure platform by 2030, serving over 10,000 organizations globally and setting the standard for developer experience in cloud-native tooling.

## 3. Our Story

NovaTech Solutions was founded in 2018 by **Sarah Chen** and **Marcus Rodriguez** in a small co-working space in San Francisco's SoMa district. Sarah, a former Principal Engineer at AWS, and Marcus, a platform engineering leader from Stripe, shared a frustration: enterprise cloud tooling was unnecessarily complex, poorly documented, and hostile to developer workflows.

They started with a single product — a Kubernetes deployment orchestrator that simplified multi-cluster management — and quickly gained traction with mid-market engineering teams. Within 18 months, NovaTech had its first 50 paying customers and a growing reputation for exceptional developer documentation and responsive support.

## 4. Core Values

### Transparency
We default to open. Information flows freely across teams. We share company financials in quarterly all-hands meetings. We write public post-mortems for customer-impacting incidents. When in doubt, we over-communicate.

### Ownership
If you see a problem, it's your problem. We don't say "that's not my job." Every engineer has production access and on-call responsibility. Every team member is empowered to make decisions within their domain without seeking excessive approval.

### Continuous Learning
We invest $2,000 per employee per year in professional development. We host weekly "Tech Talks" where engineers present on topics they're passionate about. We celebrate failure as a learning opportunity through blameless post-mortems.

### Customer Obsession
Every employee — not just sales and support — spends time reading customer feedback. Engineers participate in monthly "Customer Shadow" sessions. Our NPS score of 72 is a point of pride, and we review it at every board meeting.

## 7. How We Work

- **Communication:** Slack is our primary communication tool. Email is for external communication only. Important decisions are documented in Confluence.
- **Meetings:** We protect "Focus Time" on Wednesdays — no meetings allowed. All recurring meetings must have an agenda shared 24 hours in advance.
- **Planning:** We run 6-week cycles with 2-week cooldown periods for technical debt and exploration projects.
- **All-Hands:** Monthly company-wide all-hands every first Thursday. Recorded and posted to Confluence for those who can't attend live.

## 8. Diversity and Inclusion

NovaTech Solutions is committed to building a workforce that reflects the diversity of the communities we serve. As of January 2025, 42% of our workforce identifies as female or non-binary and 38% of engineering roles are held by underrepresented minorities. We partner with Code2040, /dev/color, and Women Who Code for recruiting and mentorship. Every employee completes annual unconscious bias training. Our Employee Resource Groups (ERGs) include Women@Nova, Pride@Nova, Black@Nova, Parents@Nova, and Veterans@Nova.

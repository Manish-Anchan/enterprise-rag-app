# NovaTech Solutions — Code of Conduct

**Last Updated:** January 15, 2025
**Document Owner:** Legal & Compliance

---

## 1. Introduction

NovaTech Solutions is committed to fostering an inclusive, respectful, and professional work environment for all employees, contractors, and visitors. This Code of Conduct sets the standard for professional behavior and outlines our expectations for how we treat one another, our customers, and our partners. Every employee is expected to read, understand, and adhere to this Code. Violations may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.

## 2. Professional Behavior Expectations

All NovaTech employees are expected to conduct themselves with integrity, professionalism, and respect. This includes:

- **Treating all colleagues with dignity and respect**, regardless of their role, level, background, or personal characteristics.
- **Communicating constructively** — especially during disagreements. Critique ideas, not people. Written communication (Slack, email, code reviews) should be thoughtful and assume positive intent.
- **Being punctual and prepared** for meetings and commitments.
- **Representing NovaTech professionally** in all external interactions, including customer meetings, conferences, and online forums.
- **Taking responsibility** for your actions and their impact on others.

## 3. Anti-Harassment Policy

NovaTech Solutions maintains a **zero-tolerance policy** for harassment of any kind. Harassment includes, but is not limited to:

- **Sexual harassment:** Unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, sexually suggestive comments, inappropriate physical contact, or displaying sexually explicit material.
- **Verbal harassment:** Slurs, derogatory comments, insults, threats, or intimidation directed at an individual or group based on any protected characteristic.
- **Physical harassment:** Unwanted physical contact, blocking movement, or physically intimidating behavior.
- **Visual harassment:** Displaying offensive images, cartoons, or gestures in the workplace or in digital communication.
- **Cyber harassment:** Harassing behavior through email, Slack, social media, or any digital platform, including persistent unwanted messaging.

Harassment is prohibited in all work-related settings, including offices, remote work environments, company events, off-site meetings, business travel, and online company spaces.

## 4. Diversity and Inclusion Commitment

NovaTech Solutions believes that diverse teams build better products. We are committed to creating an environment where every employee can bring their authentic self to work. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic.

Our commitment includes active investment in recruiting from underrepresented communities, providing equitable career advancement opportunities, supporting Employee Resource Groups (ERGs), conducting annual pay equity audits, and requiring unconscious bias training for all employees and hiring managers.

## 5. Conflict of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of NovaTech Solutions.

- **Outside employment:** Employees may hold outside employment or consulting roles only if they do not compete with NovaTech, do not use NovaTech resources, and do not interfere with job performance. Outside employment must be disclosed to your manager and People Operations.
- **Financial interests:** Employees must disclose any financial interest in a company that is a competitor, supplier, or customer of NovaTech.
- **Hiring and supervision:** Employees may not directly supervise or be involved in hiring decisions for immediate family members or romantic partners.
- **Gifts and entertainment:** Employees may not accept gifts valued over $100 from vendors, partners, or customers without VP approval.

When in doubt about a potential conflict, disclose it to your manager or the Legal team. Transparency eliminates most conflict-of-interest concerns.

## 6. Confidentiality and NDA Obligations

All employees sign a Confidentiality and Invention Assignment Agreement (CIAA) upon hire. Key obligations include:

- **Protecting confidential information:** Customer data, source code, financial data, product roadmaps, and strategic plans are confidential. Do not share them with anyone outside NovaTech without explicit authorization.
- **Data handling:** Follow the data classification guidelines in the Information Security Policy. Never store Confidential or Restricted data on personal devices or personal cloud accounts.
- **Post-employment:** Confidentiality obligations survive termination of employment. Proprietary information must not be disclosed or used after leaving NovaTech.
- **Open source:** Contributions to open-source projects that relate to NovaTech's business must be approved by your manager and the Legal team before submission.

## 7. Social Media Guidelines

Employees are welcome to use social media in their personal capacity, subject to the following guidelines:

- **Do not speak on behalf of NovaTech** without explicit authorization from the Marketing team. Add a disclaimer such as "Views are my own" when discussing industry topics that could be associated with NovaTech.
- **Do not disclose confidential information**, including unreleased product features, internal discussions, financial data, or customer information.
- **Do not disparage** NovaTech, its employees, customers, or competitors on social media.
- **Be thoughtful:** Remember that your online presence can reflect on NovaTech, even on personal accounts.

## 8. Reporting Mechanisms

NovaTech Solutions provides multiple channels for reporting concerns, violations, or suspected misconduct:

### Reporting Channels
1. **Your Manager:** For issues you feel comfortable raising with your direct manager.
2. **People Operations:** Contact people@novatech.io or any member of the People Ops team directly.
3. **Anonymous Ethics Hotline:** Call **1-888-NOVA-ETH (1-888-668-2384)** or submit a report at ethics.novatech.io. Reports are completely anonymous — the system does not track caller ID, IP addresses, or any identifying information.
4. **Legal Team:** For concerns involving potential legal violations, contact legal@novatech.io.

### Non-Retaliation Policy
NovaTech Solutions strictly prohibits retaliation against any employee who makes a good-faith report of a violation or participates in an investigation. Retaliation is itself a violation of this Code and will result in disciplinary action.

## 9. Investigation Process

When a report is received, NovaTech follows a structured investigation process:

1. **Acknowledgment:** The reporting party receives acknowledgment within 24 hours (48 hours for anonymous reports via the hotline).
2. **Assessment:** People Operations and/or Legal assess the severity and assign an investigator. The investigator will be someone with no conflict of interest in the matter.
3. **Investigation:** Interviews with relevant parties, review of evidence (Slack messages, emails, etc.). All parties are expected to cooperate fully.
4. **Findings:** The investigator presents findings to the VP of People Operations and, if necessary, the Legal team.
5. **Resolution:** Appropriate corrective action is determined and implemented. The reporting party is informed of the outcome (to the extent permitted by privacy considerations).

Investigations are typically completed within **30 business days**. Complex cases may take longer, and the reporting party will be kept informed of progress.

## 10. Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action, which may include verbal warning, written warning, mandatory training or counseling, suspension without pay, demotion, or termination of employment. The severity of the consequence will be proportional to the severity of the violation and will take into account the employee's history and any mitigating circumstances.

For questions about this Code of Conduct, contact Legal & Compliance at legal@novatech.io or People Operations at people@novatech.io.

# NovaTech Solutions — Compensation & Benefits Guide

**Last Updated:** March 1, 2025
**Document Owner:** Total Rewards Team

---

## 1. Overview

NovaTech Solutions is committed to offering competitive, equitable, and transparent compensation packages that attract and retain top talent. Our total compensation philosophy is designed to reward performance, incentivize long-term commitment, and support employee well-being. This guide covers base salary, equity, bonuses, health benefits, retirement, and perks for all full-time employees.

## 2. Salary Bands by Level

NovaTech uses a leveling framework from L3 (Junior) to L8 (Distinguished). Salary bands are benchmarked annually against market data from Radford, Levels.fyi, and Pave. All figures represent annual base salary in USD.

### Engineering Salary Bands

| Level | Title | Salary Range | Typical Experience |
|-------|-------|-------------|-------------------|
| L3 | Software Engineer | $95,000 — $130,000 | 0-2 years |
| L4 | Software Engineer II | $130,000 — $170,000 | 2-5 years |
| L5 | Senior Software Engineer | $170,000 — $220,000 | 5-8 years |
| L6 | Staff Engineer | $220,000 — $280,000 | 8-12 years |
| L7 | Senior Staff Engineer | $280,000 — $350,000 | 12+ years |
| L8 | Distinguished Engineer | $350,000+ | 15+ years, exceptional impact |

Non-engineering roles (Product, Sales, Marketing, etc.) have equivalent leveling with comparable salary ranges adjusted for function-specific market data. Contact the Total Rewards team for details on non-engineering bands.

### Geographic Adjustment
Salaries may be adjusted based on the employee's primary work location. The bands above reflect San Francisco/Bay Area compensation. Austin-based employees are on the same bands. Bangalore-based employees receive INR-denominated offers benchmarked to the top 25th percentile of the Indian tech market.

## 3. Equity Compensation

All full-time employees receive equity as part of their total compensation package.

- **Type:** Incentive Stock Options (ISOs) for U.S. employees; Non-Qualified Stock Options (NSOs) for international employees.
- **Vesting Schedule:** 4-year vesting with a **1-year cliff**. After the 1-year cliff, 25% of the grant vests. The remaining 75% vests monthly over the next 36 months.
- **Grant Size:** Varies by level. Typical initial grants range from 5,000 options (L3) to 100,000+ options (L7/L8). Exact grant sizes are determined during the offer process and are communicated in your offer letter.
- **Exercise Window:** If you leave NovaTech, you have **90 days** to exercise vested options. We are exploring extending this window for long-tenured employees.
- **Refresh Grants:** High-performing employees (Exceeds Expectations rating) are eligible for annual equity refresh grants, typically awarded during the February compensation cycle.
- **409A Valuation:** The company conducts an independent 409A valuation annually. The most recent fair market value (FMV) and valuation date are available from the Total Rewards team upon request.

## 4. Annual Bonus

All full-time employees are eligible for an annual performance bonus, paid in March following the performance review cycle.

- **Target Bonus:** Ranges from **10% to 20%** of base salary depending on level. L3-L4: 10% target. L5-L6: 15% target. L7-L8: 20% target.
- **Payout Factors:** Bonus payout is determined by two factors: individual performance rating (weighted 60%) and company performance against annual revenue targets (weighted 40%).
- **Payout Range:** Actual payout can range from 0% to 150% of the target bonus. An "Exceeds Expectations" rating combined with strong company performance can result in a payout above target.

## 5. Health Insurance

NovaTech Solutions offers three medical plan options. **The company covers 90% of the monthly premium** for employees and 75% for dependents.

| Plan | Monthly Employee Cost | Deductible | Out-of-Pocket Max |
|------|--------------------|------------|-------------------|
| **PPO (Preferred)** | ~$45/month | $500 individual / $1,000 family | $3,000 / $6,000 |
| **HMO** | ~$25/month | $250 individual / $500 family | $2,000 / $4,000 |
| **HDHP + HSA** | ~$15/month | $1,500 individual / $3,000 family | $4,000 / $8,000 |

The HDHP plan is paired with a Health Savings Account (HSA). NovaTech contributes **$750/year** (individual) or **$1,500/year** (family) to your HSA.

### Dental and Vision
- **Dental:** 100% company-paid. Covers preventive care, basic procedures, and orthodontia (up to $2,000 lifetime max for orthodontia).
- **Vision:** 100% company-paid. Covers annual eye exams, $200 allowance for frames or contacts annually.

## 6. Retirement — 401(k)

NovaTech offers a 401(k) retirement plan through Fidelity Investments.

- **Company Match:** NovaTech matches **100% of employee contributions up to 4%** of base salary.
- **Vesting:** Company match is **immediately vested** — there is no vesting schedule for the match.
- **Contribution Limits:** Per IRS limits ($23,000 for 2025, plus $7,500 catch-up for employees 50+).
- **Enrollment:** New employees are automatically enrolled at a 6% contribution rate. You may adjust your contribution percentage at any time through Fidelity.

## 7. Additional Benefits and Perks

### Learning & Development Budget
Every employee receives **$2,000 per calendar year** for professional development. This covers online courses (Coursera, Udemy, O'Reilly), conference registration fees, books, and certification exams. Submit receipts through Expensify. Unused learning budget does not roll over.

### Wellness Benefit
**$100 per month** reimbursement for wellness-related expenses. This includes gym memberships, fitness classes, therapy or counseling sessions, meditation apps (Headspace, Calm), massage, or any activity that supports your physical or mental well-being. This is automatically added to your paycheck.

### Cell Phone Reimbursement
**$75 per month** reimbursement for your personal cell phone plan, automatically added to your paycheck.

### Additional Perks
- **Commuter Benefits:** Pre-tax commuter benefits for transit and parking (up to IRS limits).
- **Life Insurance:** Company-paid basic life insurance equal to 1x annual salary (up to $500,000). Supplemental coverage available at employee's cost.
- **Disability Insurance:** Short-term (60% of salary for up to 12 weeks) and long-term (60% of salary) disability insurance, company-paid.
- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** Free, confidential counseling and support services for employees and their families (up to 8 sessions per year).
- **Referral Bonus:** $5,000 for successful employee referrals ($10,000 for L6+ engineering hires).

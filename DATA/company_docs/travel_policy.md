# NovaTech Solutions — Travel & Conference Policy

**Last Updated:** March 5, 2025
**Document Owner:** Finance Operations

---

## 1. Overview

This policy provides guidelines for employees incurring business-related travel and conference expenses. NovaTech Solutions supports travel that drives business outcomes — customer meetings, conferences, team off-sites, and cross-office collaboration. All employees are expected to exercise sound judgment and book cost-effective options. This policy applies to all 500+ employees across our San Francisco, Austin, and Bangalore offices.

## 2. Pre-Travel Approval

All business travel must be approved before booking.

- **Platform:** All travel requests and bookings must be submitted through **Concur** (concur.novatech.io).
- **Lead Time:** Submit travel requests at least **2 weeks in advance** of the departure date to secure optimal pricing.
- **Approval Routing:** Domestic travel requires direct manager approval. International travel requires manager AND VP approval.
- **Urgent Travel:** For travel required with less than 2 weeks notice (e.g., urgent customer escalation), manager can approve via email or Slack. Submit the formal Concur request retroactively within 48 hours.

## 3. Air Travel

Employees should book the most logical, cost-effective routing available.

| Flight Duration | Class Permitted |
|----------------|----------------|
| Under 6 hours | Economy |
| Over 6 hours (continuous flight time) | Premium Economy |
| Any duration | First Class and Business Class are **NOT permitted** |

### Guidelines
- Book flights at least **14 days in advance** when possible to secure better fares.
- Use company-preferred airlines listed in Concur to leverage negotiated corporate rates.
- Select refundable or changeable fares for trips that may be rescheduled.
- Frequent flyer miles earned on business travel are the employee's to keep.
- Seat upgrades purchased with personal funds or miles are permitted.

## 4. Hotels

NovaTech covers the cost of standard hotel accommodations during approved business travel.

| Location | Maximum Nightly Rate |
|----------|---------------------|
| Domestic | $250 per night (excluding taxes and fees) |
| International | $350 per night (excluding taxes and fees) |

### Exceptions for High-Cost Cities
In exceptionally high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, Zurich), employees may exceed the nightly cap by up to **20%** if no suitable options are available within the standard limit. Document the lack of availability in your Concur booking notes.

### Guidelines
- Book hotels through the Concur corporate portal to access negotiated rates.
- Choose hotels close to the meeting venue to minimize ground transportation costs.
- Extended stays (5+ nights) should use Concur's extended-stay options for better rates.

## 5. Meals and Per Diem

NovaTech uses a fixed **per diem** allowance for meals during business travel, simplifying expense reporting.

| Location | Per Diem (Daily) |
|----------|-----------------|
| Domestic | $75 per day |
| International | $100 per day |

### Per Diem Rules
- Per diem covers all meals, snacks, tips, and incidentals (e.g., bottled water, laundry).
- **Receipts are NOT required** for per diem claims.
- Per diem is **prorated** for partial travel days: 75% on departure and return days.
- If a meal is provided (e.g., conference lunch, client dinner), reduce the per diem for that meal accordingly.

### Client Entertainment
Client meals are expensed separately from per diem. Include itemized receipts and the names and companies of all attendees. The per diem should not be claimed for meals expensed as client entertainment.

## 6. Ground Transportation

- **Preferred:** Public transit (trains, subways, buses) and rideshare services (Uber, Lyft) are preferred.
- **Rental Cars:** Only when public transit and rideshare are unavailable or impractical. Requires **Director approval**. Book standard or mid-size vehicles. Decline insurance if using the company corporate card (Brex includes rental car coverage). Return with a full tank.
- **Personal Vehicle:** If using your personal vehicle, claim mileage at the IRS rate ($0.67/mile for 2025). Regular commuting to your assigned office is NOT reimbursable.
- **Taxis:** Acceptable when rideshare is unavailable. Keep receipts for fares over $25.

## 7. Conference Attendance

Attending industry conferences supports professional development and NovaTech's brand presence.

### Budget
- Each department receives a conference budget of **$5,000 per team per quarter**.
- Managers allocate this budget across team members based on relevance and impact.
- An individual employee may attend a maximum of **2 company-sponsored conferences per calendar year**.

### Registration
- Submit conference requests through Concur with: conference name, dates, location, registration cost, and a brief justification explaining business value.
- Early-bird registration is strongly encouraged.

### Speaker Exception
If an employee is selected to **speak, present, or sit on a panel** at a conference on behalf of NovaTech:
- All travel, lodging, and registration are fully covered by a **central corporate marketing budget** (not the team's quarterly allocation).
- Preparation time counts as regular working hours.
- This does **not** count against the 2-conference annual limit.
- Coordinate with Marketing for slide templates, messaging guidelines, and social media promotion.

## 8. International Travel

International travel requires additional preparation:

### Visa and Passport Support
- People Operations provides visa invitation letters and support documentation upon request.
- Visa application fees and expedited passport renewal fees (when required for company travel) are reimbursable.
- Submit requests at least **6 weeks** before travel for visa processing.

### Travel Insurance
Comprehensive international travel and medical insurance is **automatically provided** for all employees traveling outside their home country on business. Coverage includes emergency medical treatment, medical evacuation, trip cancellation, and lost luggage.

### Emergency Assistance
Employees traveling internationally must carry the **NovaTech Global Emergency Assistance card** (available from the HR portal or Concur). The card provides 24/7 access to medical and security evacuation services and includes emergency contact numbers for each region.

### Tax Implications
Working from an international location for more than 4 weeks may trigger tax obligations. Coordinate with Legal before extending any international trip.

## 9. Expense Reporting for Travel

- Submit travel expense reports through **Concur** within **14 days** of returning from travel.
- Itemized receipts are required for all expenses over $25 (except per diem).
- Combine all expenses from a single trip into one expense report.
- Include a brief business justification for the overall trip.

For questions about travel bookings or this policy, contact Finance Operations at finance@novatech.io or post in #finance-questions on Slack.

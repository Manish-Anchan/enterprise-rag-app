# NovaTech Solutions — New Employee Onboarding Guide

**Last Updated:** January 10, 2025
**Document Owner:** People Operations

---

## 1. Welcome to NovaTech

Congratulations on joining NovaTech Solutions! This guide walks you through everything you need to know for a smooth start. Your onboarding experience is designed to help you feel productive, connected, and confident within your first 90 days.

Your primary contacts during onboarding:
- **IT Help Desk:** it-help@novatech.io (Slack: #it-helpdesk)
- **People Operations:** people@novatech.io (Slack: #people-ops)
- **Your Onboarding Buddy:** Assigned before your start date (introduced via email)

## 2. Pre-Arrival Checklist (Completed by People Ops Before Day 1)

Before your first day, our People Ops and IT teams will prepare the following:

- **Equipment:** MacBook Pro 14" (M3 Pro) or equivalent Linux workstation shipped to your address or ready at your desk. External monitor, keyboard, and mouse available at your assigned desk.
- **Badge & Access:** Building access badge programmed for your assigned office. Badge photo taken on Day 1 for remote hires or pre-ordered for in-office hires.
- **Accounts Provisioned:** Okta SSO (single sign-on for all tools), Google Workspace email (firstname.lastname@novatech.io), Slack workspace access, GitHub organization membership (read-only initially), Jira and Confluence access, AWS Console access (dev environment only initially).
- **Welcome Package:** NovaTech swag bag (hoodie, water bottle, stickers, notebook) delivered or waiting at your desk.

## 4. Week 1 Milestones

By the end of your first week, you should have completed:

- All mandatory compliance training (Information Security, Code of Conduct, Anti-Harassment) in the LMS portal
- Joined all required Slack channels (see Section 6 below)
- Attended your first team standup meeting
- Had your first 1:1 with your direct manager
- Set up your local development environment following the Engineering Wiki guide
- Made at least one low-risk commit (documentation fix, config update, or test) to familiarize yourself with our PR process
- Met your onboarding buddy for at least two informal chats

## 5. 30/60/90 Day Goals Framework

### First 30 Days — Learn and Absorb
- Complete all onboarding training modules
- Understand the team's current OKRs and how your role contributes
- Shadow at least 3 team members on their daily work
- Read the top 10 pages in your team's Confluence space
- Ship your first meaningful PR (bug fix or small feature)
- Attend your first sprint planning and retrospective

### Days 31-60 — Contribute Independently
- Own a small feature or project end-to-end
- Participate in at least 2 code reviews as a reviewer
- Understand the deployment pipeline and deploy your own code to staging
- Have a career goals discussion with your manager
- Attend one cross-team meeting to understand adjacent team's work

### Days 61-90 — Drive Impact
- Deliver a project with measurable impact on team OKRs
- Contribute to architectural or design discussions
- Identify and propose one improvement to team processes or tooling
- Complete your 90-day self-assessment and review with your manager
- Begin participating in on-call rotation (with a shadow buddy for first 2 rotations)

## 7. The Buddy System

Every new hire is assigned an Onboarding Buddy — a teammate who has been at NovaTech for at least 6 months.

**Your buddy's role:**
- Meet with you at least 3 times during your first two weeks (informal, no agenda required)
- Answer the "silly questions" you might not want to ask your manager
- Help you navigate the unwritten cultural norms
- Introduce you to people outside your immediate team
- Be available on Slack for quick questions

**Your buddy is NOT:** your manager, your evaluator, or responsible for your performance. This is a peer relationship built on trust.

## 8. Mandatory Training Modules

Complete these in the LMS (learning.novatech.io) within your first 5 business days:

1. **Information Security Awareness** (45 min) — Data handling, phishing, password hygiene
2. **Code of Conduct** (30 min) — Professional behavior, reporting mechanisms
3. **Anti-Harassment Training** (60 min) — Identifying, preventing, and reporting harassment
4. **NovaTech Product Overview** (45 min) — What we build and who our customers are
5. **Engineering Onboarding** (90 min) — Architecture overview, development workflow, deployment process (engineering hires only)
